Construction

One ribbon, two chamfers, a 32 × 30 grid

The outline is a single continuous ribbon: up the stem, across the top, down the right, and back into the lower block. The channel is the one opening — where the count goes in. Every edge lands on a module line; both chamfers are a true 45°.

  • viewbox0 0 320 300 — 1 module (u) = 10 units
  • weightsstem 6u · top bar 8u · right bar 7u · channel 8.5u
  • counter19u × 12u, opening downward into the channel
  • chamferstop-left 9u, bottom-right 14.5u, both at 45°
  • corners0.7u radius on the top-right and bottom-left — the two corners the chamfers leave behind
  • accentthe lower block below the counter line. The only element that ever takes a second colour.

Redrawn from the supplied artwork. The source measured 49.1° and 42.0° on its two chamfers and had off-grid stroke weights; both were snapped, which moves 3.4% of the pixels and nothing you can see side by side. The untouched sub-pixel trace is kept at source/mark-traced-source.svg.

Clear space & minimum size

Give it the width of its own stem

Clear space on every side is 6u — the stem width, 18.75% of the mark's width. Nothing enters that band: no type, no rules, no other logo. mark-clearspace.svg has it baked in.

The mark holds down to 16px wide. At 24px and below switch to the sharpened outline — favicon.svg or the sharp prop — which drops the corner radius so the edges land on whole pixels. Below 16px, use the wordmark instead.
